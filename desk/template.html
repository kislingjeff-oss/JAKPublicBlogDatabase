    About this library

    How search works

    • Type one or more words. A post matches when every word appears in its title, tags, summary or full text. Words also match their longer forms, so colon finds colonialism.
    • Put words in quotes, such as "mutual aid", to find them together as a phrase.
    • Put a minus sign before a word, such as -Iowa, to leave out posts that use it.
    • Best match ranks title matches first, then tags, then summaries, then the rest of the text.

    Exporting

    Tick the box beside any result to add it to your selection. In the Selected & Export tab, choose a format and whether to include a summary of each article, then copy the list or download it. Rich text pastes into email and word processors with working links. Your selection is kept in this browser until you clear it.
