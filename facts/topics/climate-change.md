# Climate Change, Science and Policy

Jeff's posts on climate change, mostly from 2017–2022, follow the science (CO2 records, IPCC and U.S. assessments, temperature records) and the politics (the Paris Agreement, the Green New Deal and the Sunrise Movement, the Inflation Reduction Act, and continued fossil fuel expansion and financing). They also track extreme weather close to home, such as the 2019 Missouri and Mississippi River floods and the 2020 Iowa derecho, and disasters worldwide.
