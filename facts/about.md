# About this bundle

This bundle gathers the key historical facts that Jeff Kisling’s blog posts rely on, across 12 topics. It holds **501 facts**, confirmed by **494 outside sources** and stated in **635 posts**. The posts come from all eight of his blogs, from 2015 to 2026, including his current blog, [Channels Into Knowledge](https://channelsintoknowledge.com/). Every post can be searched in the [Journal Research Desk](https://kislingjeff-oss.github.io/JAKPublicBlogDatabase/desk/).

## How the facts were chosen and checked

- **Chosen:** for each topic, the posts were searched for specific, checkable statements about the public world: dates, events, laws, court rulings, treaties, numbers and documented actions. Facts that recur or that the posts lean on were preferred. Opinions, interpretations and private matters were left out.
- **Checked:** each fact was looked up and confirmed on an outside page: government, court, archive, university, news or the organization’s own site, with Wikipedia as a fallback. Every source link is one that was opened during the check.
- **Corrected:** 39 facts were stated slightly wrong in a post (a year, a number, a name). The bundle gives the correct version and notes what the post said.
- **Not yet verified:** 24 statements could not be checked in this pass. They are listed at the end of each topic, marked as unconfirmed.
- **Wording:** each fact is a plain summary written for this bundle. It is not a quotation from the posts.

## Limits

- This is a selection of key facts, not every factual statement in 2.5 million words of posts.
- The checks were made by an AI researcher on 2026-10-05. Sources can change or move; a few sites block automated checks, so a link that fails for a computer may still open in a browser.
- Casualty and other running figures are given with the date and the body that issued them.
