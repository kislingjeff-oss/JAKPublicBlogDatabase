# Mutual Aid, Poverty and Community Organizing

Posts trace mutual aid from Kropotkin's 1902 book and the Black Panther Party's free breakfast program to Des Moines Mutual Aid's food giveaway, bail fund and work with houseless camps, which Jeff joined in 2020. They also cover the Poor People's Campaign of 1968 and its 2018 revival under Rev. William Barber II, COVID-19's toll and economic fallout, wealth inequality, religious socialism, and community groups such as Indianapolis's Kheprw Institute.

**36 facts** checked against outside sources, 4 of them corrected (the posts had a detail slightly wrong; the corrected version is shown). Each fact lists the source that confirms it and the posts that state it. The wording is a plain summary, not a quotation from the posts.

## Facts

### 1. 1902

Russian naturalist and anarchist Peter Kropotkin published Mutual Aid: A Factor of Evolution in 1902, arguing that cooperation rather than competition is the chief factor in the evolution of species.

- **Source:** [Mutual Aid: A Factor in Evolution (Encyclopaedia Britannica)](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Mutual-Aid-A-Factor-in-Evolution)
- **In the posts:** [US Political Meltdown and Mutual Aid](https://jeffkisling.com/2020/12/27/us-political-meltdown-and-mutual-aid/) (2020-12-27); [Isn’t ‘change the system’ the answer to systemic racism?](https://jeffkisling.com/2021/01/31/isnt-change-the-system-the-answer-to-systemic-racism/) (2021-01-31); [Mutual Aid and Colonialism](https://jeffkisling.com/2021/02/08/mutual-aid-and-colonialism/) (2021-02-08)

### 2. 1918–1920

The 1918 influenza pandemic infected an estimated 500 million people, about a third of the world's population; death estimates range widely, with 25 to 50 million the generally accepted range and some estimates reaching 100 million.

> **Note:** Post quotes an email saying it 'sickened 500 million people and killed 50 million', which falls within accepted estimates.

- **Source:** [Spanish flu (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_flu)

### 3. 1967-08-16

Martin Luther King Jr. delivered 'Where Do We Go From Here?' to the Southern Christian Leadership Conference's annual convention in Atlanta on August 16, 1967, saying that asking why there are poor people leads one to 'question the capitalistic economy' and that the movement must address 'restructuring the whole of American society.'

- **Source:** [Where Do We Go From Here? (Martin Luther King, Jr. Research and Education Institute, Stanford University)](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/where-do-we-go-here)
- **In the posts:** [Martin Luther King and Capitalism](https://quakersandreligioussocialism.com/2023/01/17/martin-luther-king-and-capitalism/) (2023-01-17)

### 4. 1968-02

The Des Moines, Iowa chapter of the Black Panther Party was founded in February 1968 by Mary Rhem (later Sister Haadasha), one of the few Panther chapters established by a woman.

- **Source:** [Des Moines, Iowa chapter of the Black Panther Party (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Des_Moines,_Iowa_chapter_of_the_Black_Panther_Party); ['We wanted justice': Member of Des Moines Black Panther Party reflects on chapter's work, lessons learned (WeAreIowa (WOI-TV))](https://weareiowa.com/article/news/local/black-history/des-moines-black-panther-party-chapter-ako-abdul-samad-mary-rhem-civil-rights-bombing/524-4911f1e0-63d2-46ed-be75-5516f8e18967)

### 5. 1968-04-03

Martin Luther King Jr. gave his 'I've Been to the Mountaintop' speech at Mason Temple in Memphis on April 3, 1968, in support of striking sanitation workers; it was his last public address.

- **Source:** [I've Been to the Mountaintop (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I%27ve_Been_to_the_Mountaintop)
- **In the posts:** [Martin Luther King, Jr and Bobby Kennedy](https://quakersandreligioussocialism.com/2022/04/04/martin-luther-king-jr-and-bobby-kennedy/) (2022-04-04)

### 6. 1968-04-04

Martin Luther King Jr. was assassinated in Memphis on April 4, 1968. That evening Robert F. Kennedy announced King's death to a crowd at 17th and Broadway in Indianapolis; Indianapolis stayed calm while riots broke out in more than 100 U.S. cities. The Landmark for Peace memorial was installed near the site in 1995.

- **Source:** [Robert F. Kennedy's speech on the assassination of Martin Luther King Jr. (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_F._Kennedy%27s_speech_on_the_assassination_of_Martin_Luther_King_Jr.)

### 7. 1968-05-12

The 1968 Poor People's Campaign, announced by King at an SCLC retreat in November 1967 and led after his death by Ralph Abernathy, began with a Mother's Day march on May 12, 1968; its encampment in Washington, Resurrection City, stood from May 13 until it was closed on June 24, 1968.

- **Source:** [Poor People's Campaign (Martin Luther King, Jr. Research and Education Institute, Stanford University)](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/poor-peoples-campaign)
